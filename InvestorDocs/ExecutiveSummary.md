## The Opportunity

ScholarsGallery is an iOS platform delivering AI-powered training to Australia's 366,000 aged care workers — a workforce the 2021 Royal Commission found to be chronically undertrained in the very skills that prevent abuse, neglect, and poor outcomes: trauma-aware communication, dignity-centred dementia care, and family co-design.

Existing training solutions cost AUD $400–$1,200 per worker per year, require desktop access, and see completion rates below 30%. ScholarsGallery solves this with a subscription app at AUD $14.99/month that runs entirely on iPhone, works offline, and uses Apple's on-device AI to personalise each learning session in real time.

A second revenue stream — a Creative Studio for AI image generation and curated digital art — complements the training product and expands the addressable market to creative professionals and art collectors.

---

## Product

The app is built, tested, and live on Apple TestFlight. Core capabilities include:

- **Aged Care Monitor**: Flashcards, lesson plans, and AI Study Coach for dementia and trauma-aware care training — covering three full curriculum modules developed against Royal Commission recommendations
- **Study Coach**: Apple Foundation Models powers an on-device tutor that remembers each worker's last topic and adapts content to their role
- **Creative Studio**: AI image generation with "Dola" smart prompt assistant, curated exhibition galleries, and a digital art marketplace
- **Admin Panel**: Facility-level operator controls for B2B deployment without enterprise IT overhead
- **Full CI/CD pipeline**: GitHub Actions → TestFlight with 47 automated tests passing; App Store launch-ready

---

## Market

Australia's residential aged care workforce represents approximately 230,000 workers across 2,700+ approved providers — a mandatory training market being reshaped by post-Royal Commission government reforms. Capturing 2% of this segment (4,600 subscribers) at AUD $14.99/month generates AUD $828,000 ARR from Monitor alone.

The Creative Studio addresses Australia's 1.2M+ active art market participants and the global AI creative tools market, currently worth USD $1.4B and growing at 38% CAGR.

---

## Business Model

Revenue is subscription-based via the App Store. Apple handles billing, renewals, and fraud; ScholarsGallery retains 85% of revenue under Apple's Small Business Program. Unit economics are strong: Monitor LTV of AUD $153 against a CAC of AUD $45 (3.4× at Year 1, improving to 5.5× by Year 3 as facility partnerships reduce CAC).

## Why Now

Three forces create a rare timing window. First, post-Royal Commission regulatory pressure means aged care providers are actively seeking compliant, scalable training solutions today. Second, Apple Foundation Models (released in iOS 18, 2024) makes on-device AI personalisation possible for the first time — first movers will own the category. Third, no existing app combines AI, offline capability, clinical-grade dementia curriculum, and a native iOS experience. ScholarsGallery is built, tested, and ready to capture this gap before it closes.
