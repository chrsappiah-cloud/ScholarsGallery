ScholarsGallery is a curated digital art gallery that brings the museum experience to your pocket. Explore immersive exhibitions, discover emerging artists, generate AI-powered artwork, and build your personal collection — all wrapped in a cinematic, dark-mode-first design.

EXHIBITIONS
Browse curated exhibitions with full-bleed hero imagery, ambient audio rooms, and scholarly essays. Each exhibition is a journey through generative media, digital art, and contemporary visual culture.

AI GENERATION STUDIO
Create original artwork using AI with our built-in generation studio. Describe your vision and watch it come to life. Generated works are automatically saved to your collection with full provenance.

MEDIA LIBRARY
Import your own images, audio, documents, and videos from your device or paste external URLs. Organize your creative assets alongside curated gallery content.

SCHOLARSHIP
Read curatorial essays, explore references to leading art journals (e-flux, Artforum, Rhizome, Leonardo/MIT Press, Frieze, and more), and deepen your understanding of digital and generative art.

COLLECTOR EXPERIENCE
Acquire edition-numbered digital artworks, track your collection with certificate IDs, save favorites, and share with rich social media descriptions. Your data syncs via iCloud across all your devices.

FEATURES
• Immersive dark-mode gallery with glass-morphism UI
• AI artwork generation with auto-save to collection
• Media library for images, audio, documents, and videos
• Curated exhibitions with ambient room experiences
• Scholarly essays with links to top art journals
• Edition tracking with provenance certificates
• iCloud sync and backup
• Share artwork with rich social descriptions
